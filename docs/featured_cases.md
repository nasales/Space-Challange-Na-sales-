# Featured cases — guide for the stories (Ezequiel)

The 10 places marked in the game. Each one shows a question, a short context, charts of NASA data and,
after the player writes a conclusion, the AI checker compares it with the statistical facts.

## Where everything is
- `data/cases_index.json`: one entry per case with `id`, `title`, `place`, `lat`, `lon`, `question`
  (shown first), `context` (shown below it), `facts` (plain-English statistics used by the AI checker,
  one block per variable) and `files` (its case files).
- `data/cache/case_<id>_<VAR>.json`: one file per case and variable (`T2M`, `PRECIP`, `SOILM`) with the
  monthly series and its trend, in the format of `docs/contract.md`.
- `docs/methods.md`: method, results table, cross-dataset check against NASA POWER, and the
  weak-evidence cases.
- `docs/figures/featured_trends.png`: one-slide figure with the trend of every series.

## How to edit the texts
1. Change only the `question` and `context` fields of the case in `pipeline/cases.py`.
2. Run `python -m pipeline.build_cases --source case_export --export-dir export/case_export`. It needs
   Perry's `export/case_export` folder, which is not in git. If you do not have it, send the texts to
   Ivan and he will run it.
3. Never edit `data/cache/` or `data/cases_index.json` by hand: they are generated.

## Facts to keep honest
- Soil moisture in Doñana, Sierra de la Culebra and Tablas de Daimiel is decreasing and significant,
  but the evidence is weak (p close to 0.05). Say "probably drying", not "clearly drying".
- Temperature rises significantly in all 7 cases where it is measured.
- No precipitation trend is significant: do not say it rains more or less.
- A non-significant result means no clear evidence of change, not proof that nothing changed.
- Data credit in text only: "Data: NASA MERRA-2, GPM IMERG, GLDAS". No NASA logos.
